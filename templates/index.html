Questions, answered

Do I need to pay for Google Sheets?

No. Google Sheets is free with a Google account. You make your own copy of the template in your Google Drive.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Use the free Google Sheets app on iPhone or Android, or open it in any browser on a laptop, tablet or Chromebook.

Does it connect to my bank account?

No. You enter your own transactions, so your financial data stays private in your own Google Drive.

Can I change the categories?

Yes. Rename, add or remove categories and accounts on the Settings tab.

Will it work in Excel?

It is designed for Google Sheets. Some features, like the progress bars, will not behave the same way in Excel.
